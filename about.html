// my story

How I got here

I started in mechanical engineering. As a technician student at IFSUL, from 2020 to 2023, I worked on machine diagnostics: collecting data from equipment, analysing it and turning it into preventive maintenance plans, which I then presented at a scientific event. It was my first real lesson in data: measurements only matter when they lead to a decision.

I wanted to build the tools myself, so I moved to Computer Science at Universidade La Salle in Canoas. Over six semesters I studied data structures, algorithms and software design, and started writing my own projects in Python, Java, C and SQL. Working in technical support at Declare Cripto, a crypto-asset tax compliance company, also taught me to explain technical details clearly to people who are not engineers.

Today I live in Rouen, France, where I'm doing an MSc in Agricultural & Food Data Management at UniLaSalle and studying machine learning and deep learning. The thread has stayed the same: using data and software to solve real, physical-world problems. I'm now looking for an internship or work-study position where I can do that as part of a team.
