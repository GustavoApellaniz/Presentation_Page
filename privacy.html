Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy applies to this website, gustavoapellaniz.github.io/Presentation_Page, run by Gustavo Fraga Apellaniz as a personal portfolio and developer website.

What this website collects

  • No personal data. There are no forms, no accounts and no sign-ups.
  • No cookies. The site does not set any cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking. There are no analytics tools, advertising networks or tracking pixels.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, images and scripts are served from this site, not from external services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in a new tab) for details.

External links

This site links to GitHub and LinkedIn. Those websites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

Email

If you email me, I only use your address and message to reply to you. I do not share them with anyone.
